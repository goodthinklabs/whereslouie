Just a duck living his best life.

Louie was inspired by HitchBOT — the Canadian robot that crossed countries on the kindness of strangers. Same idea, smaller budget, better vibes.

He travels with a Tracki GPS tracker strapped to his back. His location gets logged here every 5 minutes. He has no destination. He just goes.

The only rule: if you've got him, take him somewhere interesting. Then pass him on.
