What's this all about?

Louie is a rubber duck with a Tracki GPS tracker strapped to his tiny backpack. He's inspired by Hitchbot — the Canadian robot that hitchhiked across countries relying entirely on the kindness of strangers.

Louie travels the same way. He gets passed from person to person, going wherever they take him. He doesn't have a destination. He just goes.

His position is reported over the Tracki cellular network and logged here every 5 minutes. Wherever he goes, we know where Louie is.

This project is an experiment in community, trust, and the fun of building unnecessary things. If you've got Louie, take him somewhere interesting. Then pass him on.
